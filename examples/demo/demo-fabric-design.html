1. Summary

Two independent Nexus EVPN/VXLAN fabrics, one per data center, joined by dedicated Multi-Site border gateways over a redundant routed DCI. Each site has 2 × Nexus 9500 spines (EVPN route servers), 4 × Nexus 9300 leafs in two vPC pairs and 2 × Nexus 9300 BGWs, with a Palo Alto HA pair at the north-south edge.

BGP only: eBGP IPv4 underlay on the /31 links, MP-BGP L2VPN EVPN overlay (eBGP between loopbacks), eBGP to the firewalls, across the DCI and to AWS/Azure. BFD on every session.

  • Each site keeps all infrastructure inside its own /16 (10.110.0.0/16 for DC1, 10.120.0.0/16 for DC2), in the 10.x.240.0/21 block.
  • Only routed traffic crosses the DCI. Each site has its own L2VNIs; nothing is stretched by default.
  • AWS Direct Connect and Azure ExpressRoute terminate on the BGWs in VRF TRUST at both sites.

7. Firewall integration

The firewalls connect to the border-service leafs with routed /29 links and eBGP (AS 65217 / 65227). They advertise only 0.0.0.0/0 and accept only the two site /16s, with ECMP across both leafs.
